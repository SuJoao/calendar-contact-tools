# First 30 days

## Days 0–2

Verify production from a clean browser, monitor deployment errors and CSP violations, check canonical/robots/sitemap responses, and test one fictional ICS and VCF workflow on desktop and mobile. Correct functional or privacy regressions before promotion.

## Week 1

Submit the sitemap to relevant webmaster tools, review indexing and 404 reports, answer support/security messages, and inspect only aggregate hosting logs permitted by the privacy policy. Do not enable analytics merely to fill a dashboard.

## Weeks 2–4

Review dependency updates, sponsor inventory/expiry output, browser smoke results, page performance, and search discovery. If privacy-safe analytics is deliberately enabled, document its endpoint, retention, cookie status, and consent basis first; then collect a complete period before publishing visitor claims.

At day 30, write a short review: reliability incidents, browser failures, highest-value tool feedback, verified traffic definition, sponsor conversations, donations, and the next narrowly scoped maintenance work. Do not infer user demographics from file content or fingerprint visitors.
